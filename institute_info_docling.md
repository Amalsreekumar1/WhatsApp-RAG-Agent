## Demo PSC Academy: information for students (fictional)

This document is made up for testing. It contains no prices, because prices are kept in the courses file.

### About the academy

Demo PSC Academy coaches students for Kerala PSC examinations. Classes are held in the main building. Each batch has a limited number of seats, and seats are given in the order in which admissions are completed.

The academy prepares students for the written examination, the OMR test and the online test. Teachers explain the syllabus, give weekly tests and discuss the answers in class.

### Admission process

Step 1: Visit the office or call the office number and tell us which course you want.

Step 2: Fill in the admission form. Bring one passport-size photograph and a copy of your photo identity card.

Step 3: Pay the course fee at the office. A receipt is given for every payment.

Step 4: Collect your batch time table and your student identity card on the first day of the batch.

പ്രവേശനത്തിന് ഓഫീസിൽ നേരിട്ട് എത്തുകയോ ഫോണിൽ ബന്ധപ്പെടുകയോ ചെയ്യാം. ഫീസ് അടച്ചതിന് രസീത് ലഭിക്കും.

### Class timings

Regular classes are held from Monday to Saturday, from 9:30 am to 4:30 pm. The weekend batch meets on Saturday and Sunday. A weekly test is held every Saturday afternoon.

ക്ലാസുകൾ തിങ്കൾ മുതൽ ശനി വരെ രാവിലെ 9.30 മുതൽ വൈകുന്നേരം 4.30 വരെ ആണ്.

### Hostel rules

The hostel is for students of the academy only. Students must return to the hostel before 8 pm. Visitors are allowed only in the visitors room, between 5 pm and 7 pm.

Students must keep their rooms clean and must not bring cooking equipment to the rooms. Meals are served in the dining hall at fixed times.

ഹോസ്റ്റൽ ഫീസ് മാസം തോറും ഓഫീസിൽ അടയ്ക്കണം.

### Cancellation and refund

A student who cancels admission before the batch starts receives half of the course fee back. After the batch has started, the fee is not returned.

ബാച്ച് തുടങ്ങുന്നതിന് മുമ്പ് റദ്ദാക്കിയാൽ ഫീസിന്റെ 50% തിരികെ ലഭിക്കും. ബാച്ച് തുടങ്ങിയ ശേഷം ഫീസ് തിരികെ ലഭിക്കില്ല.

### Examination notifications

The office helps students to read PSC notifications, but the official notification on the Kerala PSC website is the final source. Dates and conditions can change, so students should always check the latest notification.

### Frequently asked questions

Can I join after the batch has started? Please contact the office. Joining late depends on the seats and on the syllabus already covered.

Do you give study material? Printed notes are given for most courses. Ask the office which notes come with your course.

Is there a demo class? Please ask the office when the next demo class is held.

Can I change my batch? A change is possible if a seat is free in the other batch. Please contact the office.
